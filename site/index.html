      HOW THIS RUNS

      Nothing on this page was assembled to order. Sixteen agents each own one part of the job, a scheduler wakes them on a fixed cadence, and a monthly budget stops them. What you are reading is the output of the last run that finished.

      Collection is deterministic and owns the clock. A worker process fetches every source on a schedule, parses what comes back, matches it against what it already holds, and writes a snapshot. No model takes part in any of that, so a collection costs nothing per run and keeps going even when everything above it is stopped.

      Models are used for judgment and nowhere else. Severity, Australian relevance, impact and the editorial pass are model calls over evidence that has already been retrieved. CVSS scores, KEV status, EPSS and technique IDs are looked up from the authoritative register, never generated — which is why a field this site cannot source says unknown rather than a plausible number.

      Three collection lanes, two schedulers. A FAST lane every 15 minutes for advisories and exploitation reports; a NORMAL lane every 4 hours for news, vendor and research sources; a DEEP lane once a day at 03:00 AEST for source discovery, trends and follow-ups on developing events. The worker owns the first two, which is what makes the snapshot survive the control plane going down.

      The control plane is kept separate from the pipeline. Paperclip holds the org chart, wakes the agents, records heartbeats, and enforces budgets and approvals — and holds none of the collection logic. If it stops, collection and publication carry on without it. That split is deliberate.

      Cost is a hard limit, not a target. US$20 a month, enforced at the provider key rather than by good intentions. Ten of the sixteen agents are deterministic and spend nothing at all. One agent — RIPPERDOC — re-reads the model catalogue every morning, keeps each of the others on the cheapest model that can still do its job, prefers a free model wherever one passes the tests, and moves an agent off a model that starts failing it.
